About BristolPulse

Your central hub for discovering and booking events across Bristol.

What is BristolPulse?

BristolPulse is a web-based event management system designed to provide a unified platform for exploring events happening across the city of Bristol. Users can browse, filter, and book tickets for a variety of events including exhibitions, workshops, sports events, and more.

Our Purpose

The platform aims to enhance tourism and community engagement by making event discovery simple, accessible, and efficient. It also provides organisers with tools to manage events and track bookings.

In Collaboration with BCE

This system is developed in collaboration with Bristol Community Events (BCE), an organisation focused on promoting cultural, social, and recreational activities within Bristol. The platform supports BCE's goal of increasing event visibility and improving user experience for both attendees and organisers.

Key Features

Event Discovery

Browse upcoming and past events with filtering by category, date, and price.

Booking System

Securely book tickets, view bookings, and manage cancellations with pricing logic.

Admin Controls

Admins can manage events, monitor bookings, and generate reports.

Disclaimer

This website is a prototype developed for academic purposes. Payment functionality is simulated, and users may be required to present valid identification (e.g., student ID) at events where applicable.
